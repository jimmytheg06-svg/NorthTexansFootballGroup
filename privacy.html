Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

North Texans Football Group ("NTXN," "we," "us") runs this website as a simple, static informational site for our football club. This policy explains what little information we handle and how.

What we collect

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us directly — for example, your name, email address, and message content if you email us through the Contact section or our listed address, ntxnfootball@outlook.com.

How we use it

We use the information you send us solely to respond to your inquiry — whether that's about joining the club, sponsoring us, or a general question. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party links

This site links to third-party services we don't control, including our sponsors' websites, our social media pages, and our tournament's live scores/standings page on ClubVerse. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy doesn't cover them.

Children's information

If our programs ever involve youth players, any contact regarding those programs is expected to go through a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes as the site grows (for example, if we add analytics or a contact form later), we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at ntxnfootball@outlook.com.
