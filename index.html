Who We Are

Built on brotherhood. Driven by hard work.

North Texans Football Group is a Nepali football club at its core — founded by a group of guys who simply loved the game too much to leave it as a hobby. What began as pickup matches and shared conviction has grown into a club built on principles we don't treat as slogans: hard work, dedication, brotherhood, and respect. We carry the discipline of the pitch and the loyalty of brotherhood as values passed down, not marketing copy. Every training session, every match, and every decision we make as an organization is measured against those standards.

There are no shortcuts here and no empty talk — just effort, accountability, and a team that shows up for each other, on and off the field. That's the standard North Texans Football Group is building North Texas football on.
